===============================================================================
1. STOREFRONT HOMEPAGE (/)
===============================================================================

- Hero section with dual action CTA and dynamic gradient background.
- Live platform statistics (500+ products, 8 categories, 100% verified vendors).
- Interactive category selector with category visual cards.
- Featured products carousel and promotional banners.
- Customer trust elements (Secure transactions, Fast delivery, Buyer protection).

===============================================================================
2. PRODUCT DISCOVERY & FILTERING (/products)
===============================================================================

- Dynamic Faceted Sidebar:
  * Keyword search with real-time debounced query execution.
  * Category checklist selection.
  * Price range slider / min-max inputs.
  * Sorting algorithms: Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low, Newest arrivals.
- Responsive 3-column product grid with image zoom hover effects and stock status badges.
- Smooth skeleton loaders (ProductCardSkeleton) during network latency.
- Pagination controls with items-per-page selector.

===============================================================================
3. PRODUCT SHOWCASE (/products/[id])
===============================================================================

- Breadcrumb navigation: Home > Category > Product Name.
- High-definition image gallery with thumbnail carousel.
- Vendor attribution badge linking to the verified seller's shop info.
- Stock availability indicator (In Stock vs Out of Stock warnings).
- Interactive quantity counter (+ / -) with boundary validation against stock.
- Single-click 'Add to Cart' with instant toast notification.

===============================================================================
4. SHOPPING CART MANAGEMENT (/cart)
===============================================================================

- Real-time cart state synchronization with backend API.
- Itemized card rows showing product thumbnail, unit price, quantity controls, and subtotal.
- Single-click line item deletion.
- Summary Calculation Card:
  * Subtotal calculation.
  * Tax estimate and free shipping threshold.
  * Estimated total.
- Direct CTA to secure checkout.

===============================================================================
5. ATOMIC CHECKOUT & ORDER PLACEMENT (/checkout)
===============================================================================

- Delivery Address capture with pre-filled default from profile.
- Order review accordion showing line items and seller attribution.
- Atomic checkout processing:
  * Validates stock availability for each item.
  * Decrements product inventory in database.
  * Creates Order and OrderItem records attributed to respective vendors.
  * Empties customer cart upon successful submission.
  * Redirects to Order Confirmation page with order ID.

===============================================================================
6. ORDER TRACKING & LIFECYCLE HISTORY (/orders)
===============================================================================

- Comprehensive order cards showing:
  * Order ID, timestamp, and delivery destination.
  * Color-coded status badge for all 6 lifecycle stages:
    - Pending (Order submitted)
    - Confirmed (Order accepted)
    - Processing (Inventory prepared)
    - Shipped (In-transit)
    - Delivered (Completed)
    - Cancelled (Voided)
  * Breakdown of ordered items, vendor names, quantities, and price at purchase.

===============================================================================
7. CUSTOMER ACCOUNT & PROFILE (/account)
===============================================================================

- Personal details editor (Full Name, Phone, Shipping Address).
- Avatar image upload (via ImageUploadInput) or external profile image URL.
- Security tab for password modification with current password verification.
