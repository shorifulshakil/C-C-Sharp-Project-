DEALER (VENDOR) WORKSPACE FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
==================================================

Role: Dealer
Primary Demo Account: dealer1@test.com / Dealer@123 (Alex Tech - AlexTechs Shop)
Workspace Base Route: /dealer/*

===============================================================================
1. VENDOR HEADQUARTERS DASHBOARD (/dealer/dashboard)
===============================================================================

- High-Level KPI Summary Cards:
  * Total Products in Catalog
  * Approved & Published Products (Active in Storefront)
  * Products Pending Moderation (Awaiting Admin Review)
  * Rejected Products (Flagged for Review)
  * Financial Performance: Total Revenue ($), Total Orders, Units Sold.
- Live Storefront Status Indicator: Displays dealer verification state (Approved vs Pending).
- Recent Products Table: Quick-view of recently submitted listings.
- Action Shortcuts: Quick navigation to 'Add Product', 'Sales Analytics', 'Fulfillment Orders', and 'Shop Profile'.
- Integrated LoadingProgress: Real-time dynamic visual loading feedback.

===============================================================================
2. PRODUCT CATALOG MANAGEMENT (/dealer/products)
===============================================================================

- Multi-Tab Filtering:
  * 'All Products' tab
  * 'Approved' tab (live on customer store)
  * 'Pending' tab (moderation queue)
  * 'Rejected' tab (with viewable admin rejection notes)
- Product Actions:
  * Edit listing details, pricing, inventory stock, and media.
  * Delete product with safe modal confirmation.
  * Preview live storefront presentation.

===============================================================================
3. NEXT-GEN PRODUCT CREATOR (/dealer/products/new)
===============================================================================

- State-of-the-Art Creator UI:
  * Title, Description, and Category Selection.
  * Pricing & Inventory Controls: Real-time profit margin and stock balance calculator.
  * Automatic SKU generation helper based on vendor shop initials.
  * Integrated ImageUploadInput:
    - Dual-mode image sourcing: Local file drag-and-drop / file picker (encoded to Base64) OR external CDN URL.
    - Real-time thumbnail preview with remove/replace capability.
- Moderation Lifecycle Safeguards:
  * Products are automatically initialized with ApprovalStatus = 'Pending'.
  * Notifies the vendor that listings require administrator moderation before becoming public.

===============================================================================
4. SALES & REVENUE ANALYTICS (/dealer/sales)
===============================================================================

- Aggregate Financial Metrics:
  * Cumulative Revenue earned across all fulfilled order items.
  * Total Distinct Orders and Total Units Sold.
- Product Sales Breakdown:
  * Itemized table of best-selling products with thumbnail, unit price, total volume, and gross revenue.
  * Expandable Customer Breakdown:
    - Clicking any product reveals the list of customers who bought it.
    - Displays buyer name, email address, order timestamp, quantity purchased, and subtotal.

===============================================================================
5. ORDER FULFILLMENT & LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT (/dealer/orders)
===============================================================================

- Vendor-Specific Order Ingestion:
  * Displays orders containing line items fulfilled by this dealer.
  * Breakdown of product items, quantities, shipping addresses, and order timestamps.
- Direct Status Transitions:
  * Vendor can update order status: e.g., 'Confirm' -> 'Processing' -> 'Shipped'.
  * Strict Finite-State Machine (FSM) validation prevents invalid status leaps.
  * Validates dealer ownership so vendors can only manage orders containing their own items.

===============================================================================
6. VENDOR CUSTOMERS ROSTER (API: GET /api/dealers/customers)
===============================================================================

- Access a consolidated roster of all distinct customers who have ordered from this shop.
- Displays buyer contact information, total orders placed, and cumulative spend.

===============================================================================
7. SHOP PROFILE & BRANDING (/dealer/profile)
===============================================================================

- Redesigned Profile Management:
  * Shop Identity: Shop Name, Category, Description, and Physical Address.
  * Owner Identity: Full Name, Phone, and Email.
  * Media Management:
    - Personal Owner Avatar upload (via ImageUploadInput).
    - Shop Branding Logo upload.
  * Real-time preview of vendor branding card.
