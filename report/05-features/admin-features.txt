ADMINISTRATOR WORKSPACE FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
=================================================

Role: Admin
Primary Demo Account: admin@ecommerce.com / Admin@123 (System Admin)
Workspace Base Route: /admin/*

===============================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE PLATFORM DASHBOARD (/admin/dashboard)
===============================================================================

- Real-Time KPI Metric Cards:
  * Total Platform Users (Combined count)
  * Verified Dealers & Active Customers
  * Total Catalog Products & Pending Moderation Queue Counter
  * Cumulative Gross Revenue ($) and Total Platform Orders
- Moderation Queue Alert: Instant notification banner when new vendor products are awaiting review.
- Recent Registrations: Quick inspection of newly registered shops and buyers.
- Integrated LoadingProgress: Animated multi-stage loading feedback.

===============================================================================
2. GROUPED PRODUCT MODERATION QUEUE (/admin/products)
===============================================================================

Core Academic / Architecture Highlight: Dealer Product Approval Workflow
- Grouped-by-Dealer Moderation Interface:
  * Rather than a flat, disconnected list, pending products are intelligently grouped by Dealer / Shop Name.
  * Allows administrators to evaluate a vendor's catalog holistically.
- Verification & Quality Review:
  * Product name, category taxonomy, description, pricing sanity, and stock.
  * High-resolution image preview thumbnail modal.
- Moderation Actions:
  * Single-Click Approval: Sets ApprovalStatus = 'Approved', PublishedAt = NOW(). Product immediately goes live on storefront.
  * Rejection with Reason: Opens modal requiring administrative feedback (e.g., 'Pricing violates policy', 'Images low quality'). Sets ApprovalStatus = 'Rejected' and saves RejectionReason for dealer remediation.

===============================================================================
3. COMPREHENSIVE DEALER ADMINISTRATION (/admin/dealers)
===============================================================================

- Vendor Directory & Search:
  * Full search by shop name, owner name, or email.
  * Category filter dropdown (Electronics, Clothing, Books, etc.).
- Approval & Verification Engine:
  * Single-click instant approval toggle for pending dealer applications.
- Extended Dealer Detail Modal (Size 3xl/4xl):
  * Inspect complete shop information, address, logo, and owner credentials.
  * Embedded Customer Roster: Calls 'GET /api/admin/dealers/{id}/customers' to display all buyers who purchased from this dealer.
- Direct Dealer Creation & Edit Modal:
  * Allows admin to manually onboard, edit, or remove vendor accounts.

===============================================================================
4. CUSTOMER ACCOUNT DIRECTORY (/admin/users)
===============================================================================

- Customer Accounts Overview:
  * Full list of registered customer accounts with contact details and registration dates.
- Account Moderation Controls:
  * Toggle active status (Ban / Unban) with instantaneous API synchronization.
  * Prevents blocked users from logging in or placing new orders.

===============================================================================
5. TAXONOMY & CATEGORY MANAGER (/admin/categories)
===============================================================================

- Category Hierarchy Tree:
  * List, create, update, and delete product categories.
  * Parent-child category relationship management.
  * Referential integrity checks prevent deleting categories containing active products.

===============================================================================
6. PLATFORM ANALYTICS & REPORTS (/admin/stats)
===============================================================================

- Financial & catalog summary metrics.
- Vendor contribution metrics and revenue velocity.

===============================================================================
7. ADMIN PROFILE & SECURITY (/admin/profile)
===============================================================================

- Administrator identity and avatar image upload.
- Master credentials and password change controls.
