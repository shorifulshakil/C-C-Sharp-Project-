MASTER SEED DATA SUMMARY & STRATEGY
=====================================

The database is seeded using the master migration script: SQL/master.sql
Host: Supabase PostgreSQL (AWS ap-south-1)
Seed Version: 3.0 (Clean Master Demo Configuration)

Design Philosophy of the Master Dataset:
Rather than generating confusing, fragmented records across multiple mock vendors, the master configuration assigns 550 realistic products and all order cycles to one primary verified dealer ('Alex Tech') and one primary authenticated customer ('John Buyer'). This provides a reliable, reproducible showcase environment for live demonstrations, automated testing, and evaluation.
